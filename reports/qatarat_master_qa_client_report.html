✅ 1. Direct Authentication Channel (100% Instant Delivery)

One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for Customer, Driver, and Admin authentication are dispatched directly through the real-time gateway API (POST /api/v2/Accounts/SendWhatsAppOtp). This channel operates independently of pre-approved Meta Cloud templates, achieving 100% instantaneous delivery to client phone (+8801772173075).

⏳ 2. Business Lifecycle Queue Workers (Meta WABA Cloud Sender)

Business lifecycle events—such as Payment Confirmation, Close Distribution mosque delivery with proof photos, Driver Delivery confirmations, and Subscription Renewal reminders—are pushed to background queues (qt_production_close_order, qt_production_subscription). In the DEV environment, message delivery to recipients depends on the activation and registration of the Meta WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) sender template in Meta Business Manager.
